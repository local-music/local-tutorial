Import Your Music

Your music. Your files. All offline.

  1. 1

    Open your playlist or song

    Find the playlist or track you want in your streaming app and copy the link. This is just your reference list of what to collect.

  2. 2

    Note the tracks you want

    Save the link, or write the titles and artists down. LOCAL can't read your streaming account — this list is for you.

  3. 3

    Get copies you're allowed to keep

    For each track, use a file you already own: a download you bought, something from the artist's own store, or a copy already in your collection.

    Streaming downloads are protected and can't be imported. Using them outside their app isn't permitted by that service's terms.

  4. 4

    Save the files to Files

    Put your audio files in the iPhone Files app — On My iPhone or iCloud Drive. AirDrop works too.

    If a file sits in iCloud and hasn't downloaded yet, open it once in Files so it's stored on the device.

  5. 5

    Open LOCAL and import

    Go to your Library, tap the import button in the top corner, then choose Import Audio Files.

  6. 6

    Done — your music is in

    Pick your files and LOCAL copies them in, reading the title, artist, album and artwork from each one. Add them to a playlist and you're finished.

Supported formats

MP3 M4A AAC WAV AIFF CAF MP4 MOV
Importing several songs at once

In the Files picker you can select as many files as you like in one go. LOCAL imports them one at a time and shows a progress indicator.

If a file fails, LOCAL names it so you know which one to retry.

Something didn't import

Nothing appeared. The file may still be downloading from iCloud, or the picker returned a folder rather than files.

"Unsupported format". Convert the file to one of the formats listed above first.

A song won't play. Delete it from LOCAL and import it again from the original file.

Missing title or artwork. The file has no embedded metadata. LOCAL never invents artist, album or artwork — the song still plays.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every imported file is stored on your iPhone, so playback works on a plane or anywhere with no signal.

Lyrics are the one exception — the first lookup needs a connection, then it's cached for offline use.

Please read Only import music you own or have permission to use. Respect copyright and the terms of your music providers. LOCAL does not download music, bypass protection on streaming services, or provide access to catalogues.
